About this project

Author

Amal Mehta, independent researcher.

What this is

A small, reproducible study of training quantum neural networks with TensorFlow Quantum. It reproduces known behaviour (a strong classical baseline, the effect of a classical head, barren plateaus and noise) with current open-source tools, error bars over seeds, and code that regenerates every number. None of the findings are new; the aim is to make them easy to see, rerun and extend.

How it was made

An AI assistant (Claude) wrote the experiment code, ran the experiments, generated the figure and table scripts, drafted the paper and this site, and checked the references against arXiv and Crossref. The author chose the scope and the experiments.

Built with

TensorFlow Quantum 0.7.6, TensorFlow 2.18.1 and Cirq 1.5.0, simulated on CPU in Docker. Data: MNIST (LeCun et al., 1998). The model follows Farhi & Neven (2018); the barren-plateau setup follows McClean et al. (2018). Full references.
